Socket & port replacement

Broken sockets replaced, not whole boards

Charging and connection sockets take the most physical abuse on any device and are the most common board-level failure. We replace them directly on the motherboard.

DC power socket replacement

Laptop only charges when the cable is held at an angle, the jack feels loose, or nothing happens when you plug in. We desolder the worn DC-in socket and fit a new one — on Windows laptops, Chromebooks and MacBooks with a DC-in board.

USB & USB-C socket replacement

Damaged, bent or non-charging USB-C, micro-USB and USB-A ports on phones, tablets, laptops and consoles. Replaced at board level with the correct part, then tested for power and data.

HDMI port replacement

No signal, flickering picture or a physically bent HDMI port on a PlayStation 4, PS5 or Xbox. One of the most common console faults, and one we fix rather than replace the console.

Why board-level repair?

A replacement logic board from an Apple Service Centre can run to $900 or more — often more than the device is worth. A component-level repair fixes the actual fault for a fraction of that, keeps your data intact and keeps a working device out of landfill.

Frequently asked questions

My laptop only charges when I wiggle the cable — is that the socket?

Almost always, yes. The DC socket's solder joints crack from years of plugging and unplugging. Replacing the socket is a routine repair and far cheaper than a new motherboard.

Can you fix a USB-C port that's been bent or pushed in?

Usually. We remove the damaged port, clean up the pads and fit a new one, then test both charging and data. If the board pads are torn we'll tell you up front whether it's still repairable.

How long do board repairs take?

Simple socket and fuse jobs can be same day or next day. Liquid damage and complex no-power faults take longer because of diagnosis and parts. We'll give you an estimate when you book.

Is it worth repairing a device that's completely dead?

Often it is — especially if the data on it matters. Bring it in for a diagnosis and we'll give you an honest answer on cost versus replacement.
