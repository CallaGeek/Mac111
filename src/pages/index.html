New in the workshop

Broken socket? We replace it — no new board needed.

Worn DC power jacks, wobbly USB-C ports and dead HDMI outputs are the most common reasons a perfectly good device gets written off. With micro-soldering equipment and a certified technician, we replace the socket itself at a fraction of the cost of a replacement board.

See board-level repairs
  • DC power socket replacement on laptops and MacBooks that only charge at an angle, or not at all.
  • USB-C, USB & Lightning port replacement on phones, tablets, laptops and consoles.
  • HDMI port replacement on PlayStation 4, PS5 and Xbox — no signal, flickering or a bent port.
  • Liquid damage & no-power faults diagnosed and repaired at the component level.
