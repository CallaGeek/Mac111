Repair, don't replace

Apple Service Centres often quote $900+ for a new logic board. In many cases the fault is a single fuse, IC or connector that we can replace with micro-soldering — keeping your Mac, your data and most of your money.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a MacBook repair take?

Batteries, SSD upgrades and many screen replacements are done the same day. Liquid damage and board repairs typically take a few days depending on parts. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes please — we're a workshop rather than a retail store, so call ahead on 027 548 1618 and we'll make sure someone's ready for you.

Will I lose my data?

Most hardware repairs leave your data untouched. For anything involving the drive or a board repair we'll talk you through a backup first, and we offer data recovery if your Mac is already unbootable.

Can you fix older Macs?

Absolutely. We've been repairing Macs in Nelson for over 12 years and still work on models Apple no longer supports.
