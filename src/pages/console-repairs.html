PlayStation HDMI port replacement

No picture on the TV, a flickering or "no signal" message, or a port that's visibly bent after the cable was knocked. On PS4, PS4 Pro and PS5 the HDMI port is soldered directly to the mainboard — we remove it under the microscope and fit a new one, then check the HDMI encoder chip so the fix lasts.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a PS5 or PS4 HDMI repair take?

Typically one to two days, depending on parts on hand. Call ahead and we can often have the port ready for your model.

Will I lose my saved games?

No — an HDMI, power or fan repair doesn't touch your storage. If a drive has to be replaced we'll let you know beforehand.

Can I send my console in from outside Nelson?

Yes. We accept mail-in repairs from anywhere in New Zealand. Pack it well and include a note with your contact details and the fault.

Is there a warranty?

All console repairs are covered by at least a 90-day defect warranty.
