Frequently asked questions

How long does an iPhone screen repair take?

Usually under an hour. Book by phone so we can have the right screen ready when you arrive.

My iPhone got wet — what should I do?

Turn it off, don't charge it, and get it to us as soon as possible. The sooner we can clean the board, the better the odds of a full recovery.

Which iPhone models do you repair?

All of them, from older models through to the latest. Call with your model and we'll confirm parts and price.

Will my data be safe?

Screen and battery repairs don't touch your data. For water damage we'll do everything we can to bring the phone back so you can back it up.
