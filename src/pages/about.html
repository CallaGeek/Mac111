About MAC111

MAC111 Ltd is a small, locally owned and operated repair workshop at 3 Renwick Place, Nelson. For over 12 years we've been the place Nelson brings its Macs, iPhones, iPads and — increasingly — its laptops and game consoles.

A certified technician, not a sales counter

The workshop is run by a certified Mac technician with years of hands-on experience and up-to-date technical advice. There's no retail floor and no sales targets — just a bench, a microscope and a genuine interest in getting your device working again.

Because we're not paying for a shopfront, our overheads are low and our prices reflect that. We'll always tell you honestly whether a repair is worth doing.

Repair at the component level

What sets MAC111 apart is board-level repair. Micro-soldering equipment lets us replace individual sockets, fuses, connectors and chips on motherboards instead of swapping the whole board. That's how we fix DC power sockets on laptops, USB-C ports on phones and HDMI ports on PlayStations for a fraction of what a replacement would cost.

Fast, and you're welcome to watch

Many repairs are done on the spot, often in under an hour. You can sit right next to us while we fix your device. For customers outside Nelson we offer mail-in repairs from anywhere in New Zealand, with same-day return available.

Our warranty

All repairs include at least a 90-day defect warranty, and iPhone and iPad screen repairs are covered for 12 months. If there's any problem with our work in that time, we'll repair it again for free. The warranty covers defects in parts and workmanship; it doesn't cover accidental damage or liquid damage after the repair.

MAC111 is an independent repair service. We're not affiliated with Apple, Samsung, Sony or Microsoft, and we don't carry out Apple warranty repairs.
